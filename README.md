# CleanView
This mod removes the vignette effect around the edges of the screen. The vignette is the dark overlay that appears around the edges of your display.

<img width="50%" height="50%" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a43b7ab6-595c-4ad8-9e09-8db7d4769730" />


## Additional Features
### BoderVignette
This option allows you to keep the red vignette effect that appears when you get close to the world border.

<img width="50%" height="50%" alt="BorderVignette" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/09f9e424-4733-4256-bd9a-4266d17ff526" />


### CustomVignetteColor
This option allows you to customize the vignette color using RGB values.

<img width="50%" height="50%" alt="CustomVignetteColor" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7b4f0209-828c-435f-abfd-21e837616eca" />


### SpyglassEffekt
This option lets you choose whether the vignette effect should be displayed when looking through a spyglass. You can choose between the normal spyglass overlay, the vignette effect, or no overlay at all.

<img width="50%" height="50%" alt="SpyglassEffekt" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/07570137-afd2-4563-8757-120ec00c8d87" />


### EquipmentEffekt
This option lets you choose how the screen should look when wearing equipment that applies a screen overlay, such as a carved pumpkin. You can choose between the normal overlay, a vignette effect, or no overlay at all.

<img width="50%" height="50%" alt="EquipmentEffekt" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/194ccbaa-37c0-4905-bbb1-e172e4f4c4cc" />


### FreezingEffekt
This option lets you choose how the screen should look when you are freezing in powder snow. You can choose between the normal freezing overlay, a vignette effect, or no overlay at all.

<img width="50%" height="50%" alt="FreezingEffekt" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6d625569-5115-4b93-af75-4766bfd4380d" />


### PortalEffekt
This option lets you choose how the screen should look when you are standing inside a portal. You can choose between the normal portal overlay, a vignette effect, or no overlay at all.

<img width="50%" height="50%" alt="PortalEffekt" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e2c86a42-7d80-46b6-8d80-abb5895a3973" />
